# Judge demo script (Digital Bridge 2026 · Track 3)

1. Open `/savanna/pages/concept-with-navigation.html` — widget launcher visible, keyboard focusable.
2. Ask in English: "What is shaping the future of VA work?" — grounded answer + citations.
3. Ask in Amharic: "ቨርቹዋል ረዳት ምንድን ነው?" — Amharic reply (verified language).
4. Ask out of scope: "How do I bake chocolate cake?" — honest refusal + human help action.
5. Mic (if key configured): record a short English/Amharic clip — transcript editable before send.
6. Toggle offline (DevTools): ask a course question — local pack answer; pending badge on reconnect (consent before sync).
7. `/admin/ingest` — show SQLite kb_version, ingest a lesson, test a question with confidence.
8. Telegram `/start` — six languages; experimental marked; `/human` creates Jitsi ticket; staff chat notified when configured.
9. Jobs: "What jobs are available?" — only verified SQLite jobs; expired hidden.
10. `/api/health` — provider, kb_version, speech, rate_limit, usage.

Credentials stay server-side. Do not claim NVDA/TalkBack until the co-designer has signed the WCAG report.
